# erschließbar

**Meaning:** inferable; can be worked out (e.g. from context)
**Level:** B2 · **Topic:** [[T15 Sprache, Kommunikation & Denken]] · **Register:** neutral

> [!example] Beispiel

> *The meaning of the word can be inferred from the text.*

**Collocations**
