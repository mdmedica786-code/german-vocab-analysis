# darum

![[darum.ogg]]

[▶️ YouGlish](https://youglish.com/pronounce/darum/german) · [🎬 PlayPhrase](https://www.playphrase.me/#/search?q=darum&language=de)

**Meaning:** that's why
**Other senses:** for that reason; about it; that is why
**Level:** B1 · **Topic:** [[T16 Allgemein (Funktionswörter, abstrakter Wortschatz)]] · **Register:** neutral
**Note:** Also: darum bitten = to ask for it.

> [!example] Beispiel
> Max' Lautsprecher geht nicht, darum lässt er ihn reparieren.
> *Max's speaker doesn't work, so he's getting it repaired.*
