**Meaning:** there; to there; away
**Level:** A2 · **Topic:** [[T08 Reisen, Verkehr & Orte]] · **Register:** neutral
**Note:** Direction away from the speaker; her = towards the speaker.

> [!example] Beispiel
> Wo gehst du hin?/Wohin gehst du?
