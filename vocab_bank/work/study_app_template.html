How to study (5 minutes to learn, 20 minutes a day)

  1. Open the tab Study. Choose a deck, for example B2 words, and the levels you want.
  2. You see a German word. Try to remember the meaning, then click the card (or press Space).
  3. Press Again if you did not know it, Good if you knew it after a short think, Easy if it was effortless.
  4. The program shows hard words again soon and easy words after days or weeks. Do your due cards first every day; then new cards.
  5. Use the tab Search to look up any word, plural or meaning in all four banks.
  6. Open the tab Progress and save a backup file every week.

The four decks

  • Main words: about 6,500 words from A1 to C1 (includes medical words).
  • B2 words: about 2,400 extra words for B2 and daily life (no medical).
  • Phrases: about 1,000 useful phrases grouped by purpose (give an opinion, agree, give reasons).
  • Redemittel: about 1,200 sentences for the speaking and writing parts of the exam.

Tip: 20 new cards a day is a good start. If due cards pile up, lower the number of new cards.
