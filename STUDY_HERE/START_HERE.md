# START HERE

You only need **one file**: `German_Study.html`.

## 1. Open it
Double-click `German_Study.html`. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox). No internet, no installation.
Tip: bookmark it, or copy the file to your phone and open it there.

## 2. Study for 20 minutes a day
1. Tab **Study**. Open "Deck and settings" and choose a deck (see below) and your levels.
2. Look at the German word and try to remember the meaning. Click the card (or press Space).
3. Press **Again** (did not know), **Good** (knew it) or **Easy** (very easy). Keys 1, 2, 3 also work.
4. The program shows difficult cards again soon and easy cards after days or weeks.
5. Every day: first all **due** cards, then the new ones (20 new cards a day is a good start).

## 3. The four decks
| Deck | What it is | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Main words | about 6,500 words A1 to C1, with some medical words | the base for everything |
| B2 words | about 2,400 more words for B2 and daily life | after you are comfortable with B1 |
| Phrases | about 1,000 useful phrases (opinion, agreeing, reasons) | with any level |
| Redemittel | about 1,200 sentences for the speaking and writing exam | before the exam |

A simple plan: now to December: Main words (levels A2 and B1) plus Redemittel (B1). Then: B2 words plus Redemittel (B2).

## 4. Search
Tab **Search**: type any German word, plural or English meaning. It searches all four decks.

## 5. Save your progress
Your progress lives in the browser. Tab **Progress** → **Save backup file** once a week, and keep the file safe. You can load it on another computer with **Load backup file**.
