# External reading and listening materials (checked 5 Oct 2026)

**How the links were checked.** Every URL below was opened with a web-fetch tool on 5 Oct 2026 (two research passes; I then re-opened 22 of the most important ones myself and the pages matched). A level is quoted only when the page itself prints it; where a page prints no CEFR level I say so and give my own estimate in brackets. Prices are the ones shown on the page that day. Items I could not open are listed at the very end and are **not** recommended.

**One routine for every item (use it unless the row says otherwise).** After the weekly batch: read or listen once for gist; second pass with the batch list next to you and press Ctrl+F for each target word; paste the sentence where it occurs into the *example* field of the card; on day 4 listen again without the text.

## Exam-format facts used in this project (from goethe.de)

- **B1 Schreiben** (60 min in total): Aufgabe 1 an e-mail, circa 80 words; Aufgabe 2 "Schreiben Sie nun Ihre Meinung", circa 80 words (a discussion/forum contribution); Aufgabe 3 an e-mail, circa 40 words ("Entschuldigen Sie sich höflich und berichten Sie, warum Sie nicht kommen können" in the model paper). The practice page gives 20 / 25 / 15 minutes; the official total is 60 minutes. Source: [bfu.goethe.de B1 Schreiben](https://bfu.goethe.de/b1_mod/schreiben.php), [B1 Modellsatz](https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/materialien/B1/b1_modellsatz_erwachsene.pdf).
- **B1 Sprechen** (about 15 min for a pair): Aufgabe 1 *Gemeinsam etwas planen* ("machen Sie Vorschläge und reagieren Sie auf die Vorschläge"); Aufgabe 2 *Ein Thema präsentieren* (five slides); Aufgabe 3 *Über ein Thema sprechen* (give feedback on your partner's presentation and ask a question). Source: [bfu.goethe.de B1 Sprechen](https://bfu.goethe.de/b1_mod/sprechen.php).
- **A2**: Schreiben (SMS 20–30 words, e-mail 30–40 words) and Sprechen (questions, a short talk about yourself, finding an appointment together). **B2**: Schreiben 75 min (forum contribution and a formal message), Sprechen 15 min (Vortrag + Diskussion). Sources: [Goethe A2](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/prf/prf/gzsd2/wi2.html), [Goethe B2](https://www.goethe.de/ins/us/de/spr/prf/gzb2.cfm).
